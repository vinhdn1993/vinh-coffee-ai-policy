Privacy Policy - Vinh Coffee AI

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Vinh Coffee AI is a personal AI-assisted content creation and management tool operated by its owner.

Information We Access

When a user connects their TikTok account, the application may access information permitted by the user through TikTok's authorization process, including basic profile information and permissions related to video upload or publishing.

How Information Is Used

  • Connect the authorized TikTok account.
  • Create and manage content.
  • Upload videos or publish content when explicitly authorized by the user.
  • Manage the application's TikTok integration.

Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored securely and are not intentionally shared with third parties. The application uses security measures to protect stored information.

User Control

Users may disconnect their TikTok account at any time. Disconnecting removes the application's stored TikTok authorization information.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner through the GitHub repository.

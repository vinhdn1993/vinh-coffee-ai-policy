Vinh Coffee AI is a personal AI-assisted application designed to support product research, content creation, video production, and content management.

Application Features

  • Product information analysis

  • Video content creation

  • TikTok account integration through official authorization
  • Video uploading and publishing when authorized by the user

TikTok Integration

Vinh Coffee AI may connect to a TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization process.

The application uses only permissions granted by the account owner. Content publishing is performed only when authorized by the user.

Users may disconnect their TikTok account at any time.
